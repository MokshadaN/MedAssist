No reliable safeguards to prevent harmful AI errors in medical outputs.
Lack of tools to generate concise SOAP summaries for clinical assistance.
No unified system linking patient check-ins with long-term health tracking.
No automated system to ensure treatment adherence and continuity of care.
AI outputs are not validated against trusted medical databases.
No secure way to share critical patient data with first responders.
Clinical triage is not integrated with real-time hospital discovery and routing.OBJECTIVES01

\section{Results and Discussion}

\subsection{Experimental Results}

All evaluations were conducted using publicly available clinical benchmarks and standardized statistical methodologies with 95\% Wilson confidence intervals.

\subsection{Speech Recognition Accuracy Evaluation}
\label{sec:speech_recognition_eval}

The speech-input module of MedAssist was evaluated across three distinct public benchmark corpora ($N = 250$ total speech utterances):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Clinical \& Medical Symptoms Corpus ($N = 100$):} Real-world emergency triage descriptions derived from public AHRQ ESI v4 and MTSamples clinical archives.
    \item \textbf{LibriSpeech Standard Continuous Speech Benchmark ($N = 75$):} Standardized continuous speech from the OpenSLR LibriSpeech test-clean benchmark.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Accent Emergency Intake Corpus ($N = 75$):} Urgent healthcare requests synthesized across diverse global vocal accents (US, British, Indian, and Australian English) following Google FLEURS and Mozilla Common Voice protocols.
\end{enumerate}

Generated transcripts from the Whisper-based ASR pipeline were aligned against ground-truth transcriptions to calculate Word Error Rate (WER) and Character Error Rate (CER).
